Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

SleepingStudio (“the app”) is a Windows desktop application. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: SleepingStudio has no servers of its own. Your data stays on your computer, except what is sent to the AI provider you configure so it can answer you.

Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, the app asks Google only for your basic profile: name, email address and profile picture (scopes openid, email, profile). Sign-in happens on Google’s own page in your web browser; the app never sees your Google password. The app keeps your name, email and picture on your computer only, to show which account is signed in. Google access tokens are used once during sign-in and are not stored. The app does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data.

SleepingStudio’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI provider (OpenRouter)

You provide your own OpenRouter API key. It is stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows (DPAPI). When you chat, your messages — and, if connected, relevant content from your Roblox place such as script text, instance names and Output logs — are sent to OpenRouter and the AI model provider you select, so they can generate a reply. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies.

Roblox Studio connection

When enabled, the app connects to Roblox Studio on your computer through Roblox’s local MCP server to read your open place (read-only). This content is used only to answer your requests and is sent only to the AI provider as described above. The app also reads Roblox Studio’s recent-files list on your PC to show your projects.

Data stored on your computer

  • Settings, chosen AI model and theme
  • Your signed-in account’s name, email and picture
  • Your OpenRouter API key (encrypted)
  • Chat history
  • Local log files for troubleshooting (API keys and tokens are removed from logs)

These live in %APPDATA%\SleepingStudio. You can delete them at any time by signing out, removing your key in Settings, or uninstalling the app and deleting that folder.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or share your personal information, don’t use it for advertising, and don’t collect analytics or telemetry.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Younger Roblox developers should use it only with a parent or guardian.
